Before you begin.

Do I need an account?

No. Take Listening and Reading tests without one. AI Writing and Speaking evaluation needs a verified account and a plan allowance, and your past AI feedback is kept in your account.

Test Practice vs Question Practice?

Test Practice runs a full test or a single module in exam conditions. Question Practice lets you choose question types, or pick individual questions, to build a custom session.

Is AI feedback an official IELTS score?

No. It gives an estimated band to guide your practice. JumpToNine is an independent practice platform and is not the official IELTS test.
